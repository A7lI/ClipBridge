How offline sharing works

Open this page online once on each phone and wait for “Ready for offline nearby sharing”. Connect both phones to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Choose Offline nearby. One phone creates an invitation; the second scans it and shows a return QR. The first phone scans that return QR. Files then travel directly between the phones without internet. Some guest Wi-Fi networks block local connections.

Online sharing works across Wi-Fi and mobile data. Both phones must stay open and connected; there is no offline delivery queue. Invites are private and stop working when the session ends. Mobile data charges may apply.
