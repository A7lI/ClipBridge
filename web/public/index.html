Pair with your PC

In ClipBridge on Windows, open iPhone web pairing and enable it. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi.

Hold the whole iPhone web pairing code in view.

Paired with your PC

Send

Receive

Copy text, an image, or files on Windows, then tap Get from PC. For files selected in the PC app, tap Receive files.

Save the received files before closing this app.

For documents, save to Files to copy and paste them in other apps.

Add ClipBridge to your Home Screen

In Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if Safari shows the option. Open ClipBridge from the new icon, then scan the PC’s pairing QR there.
